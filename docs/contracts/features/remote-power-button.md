## Edge cases
Pointer cancellation/motion, page navigation/blur/visibility loss, authentication expiry or native selection retirement cancel a hold/dialog and fence late preparation callbacks. Release after a hold never sends a short action. Provide keyboard/context-menu access to confirmation. Display sleep/wake errors/unknown acknowledgements do not retry or optimistically claim the observed power state. A new timer/wake/input invalidates stale sleep work; previously dispatched pause may finish only on its original target. Host off cannot be remotely woken with this feature; no router/firewall/WoL/CEC provisioning. Bounded live diagnosis found HDMI-A-1 already on and no exposed CEC interface; operator confirmed the TV's own remote restored the picture. Treat its acknowledgement as a request, not observation that the TV woke. Missing hardware/tool or failed/uncertain attempt falls back to the output-on operation plus a transient out-of-flow manual-TV-remote hint. No live CEC actuation test on other hardware, no guarantee of universal TV compatibility. Do not promise restoring Beelink output always wakes a physical TV.

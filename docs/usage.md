# Use the TV and phone

The TV opens to service tiles. Select one with the remote arrows and Enter, or point and click from the phone. **Home / Плитки** returns to the tiles and closes the service tabs managed by the Firefox bridge. The small **System** control opens TV settings; it can be clicked with the pointer and is skipped by tile-arrow navigation.

Scan the QR on the TV with a phone on the same trusted home network. New installations require no PIN; if PIN is enabled, the four-digit code appears only on the TV. The phone keeps its session across page reloads. The phone **Menu → QR for second phone** shows the current address for another device. If the TV's IP changes, the phone tries to switch addresses while the old connection still works; otherwise rescan the TV QR.

Drag on the touchpad to move the TV pointer; tap to click. Edge arrows send navigation keys. After arrow or Tab navigation, a tap sends Enter to the focused item. Shift+Tab, Tab, Enter and Back sit below the pad. Two fingers scroll. **Text** opens the phone keyboard for the currently focused Firefox field. Website keyboard navigation depends on the site's own focus behavior.

The main volume slider controls the selected output. Its button reads **Вкл**, **Выкл** or **?** when state cannot be checked. **Menu → Sound** provides output selection, test chime, output and Firefox mute, and separate output, Firefox-stream and page-player levels. A Firefox stream appears only while it plays; its saved target and mute state apply to new streams. A site's own audio system can still behave differently. The TV System has the same audio settings.

When Firefox exposes an active media session, a compact panel appears on the phone and the touchpad becomes a little shorter. The panel shows the track or video title, play/pause, position and previous/next where the site supports them. Swipe a long title sideways to read it. A live stream may have no usable position; unsupported controls are disabled. The panel hides when no Firefox media session is available and never controls another application's player. This uses Firefox's Linux media controls and needs no bridge update.

The timer offers Off, 15, 30, 60, 90 and 120 minutes. The remaining time appears subtly on the TV and phone, with an actionable TV warning two minutes before expiry. At expiry the TV pauses managed playback before darkening HDMI; if it cannot establish silence, it leaves the display on and reports why. Touch or a phone command wakes the display without resuming playback.

TV System also contains PIN, cursor acceleration, cursor magnifier and Firefox proxy controls. The phone magnifier button is beside volume. Proxy changes take effect after a TV-session restart. The phone Menu can open TV System, and its Settings page holds PIN, proxy and cursor acceleration.
